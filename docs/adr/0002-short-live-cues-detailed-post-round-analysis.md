# ADR 0002: Use short live cues and detailed post-round analysis

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The training simulator can assist the apprentice comedian during a round or after it. Full suggestions during the round may look impressive, but they risk training dependency and can disrupt timing.

The product's goal is to develop the performer's comedic instincts, especially the ability to notice callbacks and contradictions under pressure.

## Decision

During a practice round, the product will show only short live cues such as "callback available", "contradiction", "strong question", or "escalate".

Detailed explanations, alternative riffs, missed opportunities, and scoring will appear in the post-round analysis.

## Consequences

The live experience remains fast and performance-like. The apprentice comedian keeps authorship of the response while still receiving perceptual guidance.

The post-round analysis becomes the main learning surface, so it must be clear, specific, and visually connected to the session's conversation graph.
